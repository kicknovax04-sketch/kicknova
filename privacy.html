Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what data Kicknova Content Tool ("the Service") accesses when you connect your TikTok account, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data we access

Only with your permission, granted on TikTok's own authorization screen, the Service may access:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar.
  • Video information: a list of your videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Video uploads: video files and captions that you choose to upload or publish through the Service.
  • Access tokens: OAuth tokens issued by TikTok so the Service can act on your behalf.

The Service does not access your TikTok password, direct messages, contacts, or payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To upload and publish the videos you choose.
  • To display your profile and video statistics back to you.

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

OAuth tokens are stored by Composio, the integration provider used to connect to TikTok, under its own security and privacy practices. Video and profile data is retrieved from TikTok when needed and is not kept longer than necessary to perform the action you requested.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok (to perform the actions you request) and Composio (to manage the connection). It is not shared with anyone else unless required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the Service at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. This invalidates the Service's access tokens. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 or under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
