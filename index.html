Kicknova Content Tool

A personal tool for managing my own TikTok content.

What it does

  • Uploads and publishes videos to the connected TikTok account.
  • Shows basic profile information for the connected account.
  • Lists the account's videos and their public stats (views, likes, comments).

How it works

You sign in with TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and approve the permissions the tool asks for. The tool then acts only on your own account and only within those permissions. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
